Environmental problems that reduce to ODEs: tank draining and mixing, gradually varied flow, stratified flow, jets and plumes.

## Learning goals

- Formulate mass-balance ODEs for tanks and natural systems
- Compute gradually varied flow profiles
- Apply integral models to jets and river plumes
