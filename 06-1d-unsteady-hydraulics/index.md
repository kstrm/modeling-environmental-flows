# 1D unsteady hydraulics

The Saint-Venant equations, their waves, the explicit and implicit schemes used in 1D river models, and an introduction to bed change.

## Learning goals

- Derive the Saint-Venant equations and identify their wave speeds
- Write the equations in conservative form and apply the Lax and MacCormack schemes
- Find the CFL limit for the shallow-water equations
- Describe the Preissmann scheme and common boundary conditions
- Write the Exner equation and name common transport closures
