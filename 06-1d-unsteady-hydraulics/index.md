# 1D unsteady hydraulics

The Saint-Venant equations and the explicit and implicit schemes used in 1D river models.

## Learning goals

- Write the Saint-Venant equations in conservative form
- Describe the Lax, MacCormack and Preissmann schemes
- List common boundary conditions for 1D channel models
