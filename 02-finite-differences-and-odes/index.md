# Finite differences and ODE initial value problems

Numerical solution of ODEs, from Euler's method to Runge-Kutta and multistep methods.

## Learning goals

- Derive finite-difference approximations from Taylor series
- Explain order of accuracy and stability
- Implement Euler, RK2 and RK4 for single ODEs and systems

3. [Runge-Kutta Methods](notes/03-runge-kutta-methods.md)
4. [Systems of ODEs](notes/04-systems-of-odes.md)
5. [Multistep Methods - Adams-Bashforth-Moulton](notes/05-adams-bashforth-moulton.md)
