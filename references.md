# References and resources

## Textbooks

- Aref, H. and Balachandar, S. (2018). *A First Course in Computational Fluid Dynamics*. Cambridge University Press.
- Wu, W. (2007). *Computational River Dynamics*. CRC Press.
- Chaudhry, M. H. (2008). *Open-Channel Flow*. Springer.
- Vreugdenhil, C. B. (1998). *Computational Hydraulics: An Introduction*. Springer.
- Bates, P. D., Lane, S. N. and Ferguson, R. I. (eds.). *Computational Fluid Dynamics: Applications in Environmental Hydraulics*. Wiley.
- Parker, G. *1D Morphodynamics of Rivers and Turbidity Currents* ([e-book](http://hydrolab.illinois.edu/people/parkerg/morphodynamics_e-book.htm)).
- Kundu, P. K. and Cohen, I. M. (2008). *Fluid Mechanics*. Elsevier.
- Fischer, H. B. et al. (1979). *Mixing in Inland and Coastal Waters*. Academic Press.
- Mathieu, J. and Scott, J. (2000). *An Introduction to Turbulent Flow*. Cambridge University Press.

## Python

- [Starting out with Python](https://github.com/kstrm/Starting-out-with-python): links and examples to get going.
- [Scientific Python lectures](https://github.com/jrjohansson/scientific-python-lectures) by Robert Johansson.

## Software documentation

- HEC-RAS manuals (US Army Corps of Engineers, Hydrologic Engineering Center).
- Delft3D-FLOW user manual (Deltares).

## Videos

- NSF series on fluid mechanics, and the University of Iowa Hunter Rouse video series (both on YouTube).
- APS Division of Fluid Dynamics, [Gallery of Fluid Motion](http://gfm.aps.org).

## Papers

- Strom, K. and Keyvani, A. (2016). Flocculation in a decaying shear field and its implications for mud removal in near-field river mouth discharges. *Journal of Geophysical Research: Oceans*.
- Socolofsky, S. A. and Jirka, G. H. (2004). *Environmental Fluid Mechanics, Part I: Mass Transfer and Diffusion*. Texas A&M University.
